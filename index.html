The vision

Every company, movement and market began as a story enough people chose to believe.

Stories are how culture shifts and how businesses become empires. They are also how frauds are sold. The difference is what is true underneath.

Now machines can tell stories endlessly and for free. That does not make stories worth less. It makes judgment worth more: knowing which story to tell, why, and whether it is true. That is the work of the next decade, and it is mine.

Principles

What defines the work.

Story is the business plan

Every growth plan is a story told out loud. I start from the business strategy, never from the campaign.

Taste, refined forever

Taste is trained like a muscle: every week, every draft, every decision about what not to ship.

Intensity

The companies that become empires care more, for longer, than anyone thought reasonable. So do the stories.

True underneath

A story is only as good as what is true beneath it. Every claim carries its proof and its limit.

Purposeful growth

Growth that a company, its people and its investors are proud of, not growth at any cost.

Joy

Growth that feels like joy is growth people choose to join. Joy is the evidence the story is true enough to enjoy telling.

Provenance

Big stories, true underneath.

The same craft that built the companies we admire built the frauds we remember. So every story starts with a person, and every amplified one carries its receipts.

Human origination

A person decides

The strategy, the position and the final word are human. AI drafts, researches and amplifies. It never decides what is true.

Proof per claim

Nothing unsourced

Every number has a source and a date. Every claim states its limit before anyone else has to.

Named owner

No unowned autonomy

Every agent has an owner, an editor gate and a kill criterion. Nothing ships because a machine thought it was fine.
